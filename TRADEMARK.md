# Beecord Trademark Policy

The source code in this repository is licensed under
[Apache-2.0](LICENSE). That license governs the **code** — it does not grant
you any rights in the project's **names and logos**.

**Nothing here is legal advice.**

## 1. What the license does not cover

Apache-2.0 §6 states that no trademark license is granted by the license. You
receive copyright and patent licenses for the code, and nothing for the
project's branding.

## 2. The marks

The names **"Beecord"**, **"Beecord Studio"**, and **"BuzzLink"**, together
with their logos and visual identity (see [docs/BRAND.md](docs/BRAND.md) and
[docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md)), refer to **this official
project only** — its maintainers, its releases, and its apps as published from
this repository.

## 3. Building products on this code

Apache-2.0 permits commercial use, including selling products built on this
codebase, closed-source modifications included (see
[docs/COMMERCIAL.md](docs/COMMERCIAL.md) for what you must handle yourself).
But when you distribute your own product, **sell it under your own brand**:

- Do not name your product or company "Beecord" or "BuzzLink", and do not use
  names or logos that are confusingly similar.
- Do not present your product as the official Beecord project, an official
  release, or an official partnership.
- Do not use the project's logo or brand assets to market your product.

If you maintain a fork, keep it clearly distinguishable: your own name and
visual identity, with a note that it is derived from or compatible with
Beecord.

## 4. Uses that are fine

Reasonable, factual (indicative) uses that do not suggest endorsement are
allowed, for example:

- Stating that your software or hardware "implements the BuzzLink protocol" or
  "is compatible with Beecord devices".
- Referring to Beecord/BuzzLink when explaining what your project is based on,
  with attribution to this repository.
- Linking to this repository and quoting its documentation.

## 5. Questions

If your intended use is not clearly covered above, open an issue and ask before
shipping. We'd rather answer a question than send a correction afterwards.
